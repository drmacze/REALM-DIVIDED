FAQ

What is Realm Divided?+

A medieval Minecraft project set on a large continent split into five rival factions after its emperor dies without an heir.

Can players choose a faction?+

Yes. The core concept is built around allowing players to align themselves with any of the five factions.

Can I pre-order now?+

The pre-order page is now available as a project gate, but checkout and payment are not open yet. Pricing and edition details will be announced later.

Which Minecraft edition will be supported?+

Final platform and version support will be announced closer to release.

When will the factions be fully revealed?+

Faction names, leaders, territories, and detailed lore will be revealed gradually during development.
