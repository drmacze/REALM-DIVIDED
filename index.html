Questions from the realm

FAQ

What is Realm Divided?+

A medieval Minecraft project set on a large continent that split into five factions after its emperor died without an heir.

Can players choose a faction?+

Yes. The core concept is built around allowing players to align themselves with any of the five factions.

Is Realm Divided available to download now?+

Not yet. The project is still in development and no public release date has been announced.

Which Minecraft edition and versions will be supported?+

Final platform and version support will be announced when the project is closer to release.

When will the five factions be fully revealed?+

Faction names, leaders, territories, and detailed lore will be revealed gradually as development progresses.
